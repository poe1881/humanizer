# Preservation examples

These are editorial examples, not verified claims about real events. Preserve the source language and the writer's level of certainty. The goal is clearer wording with the same claims.

## Indonesian: estimates and numeric bounds

Before:
> Perlu digarisbawahi bahwa pendapatan diperkirakan naik 8–12% pada 2027, jika kontrak diperpanjang. Hasil ini belum terkonfirmasi.

After:
> Pendapatan diperkirakan naik 8–12% pada 2027 jika kontrak diperpanjang. Hasil ini belum terkonfirmasi.

Do not replace the range with 10%, turn an estimate into realized growth, or drop the contract condition.

## Indonesian: audit notation

Before:
> Penting untuk dicatat bahwa selisih Rp1.250.000 belum direkonsiliasi. Volume lebih dari 3.000 liter tidak termasuk retur.

After:
> Selisih Rp1.250.000 belum direkonsiliasi. Volume lebih dari 3.000 liter tidak termasuk retur.

Do not change the currency, number separators, lower bound, negation, or exclusion.

## Indonesian: uncertainty and attributed views

Before:
> Dalam lanskap yang terus berkembang, menurut Sari, permintaan mungkin turun. Data belum menunjukkan penyebabnya.

After:
> Menurut Sari, permintaan mungkin turun. Data belum menunjukkan penyebabnya.

Do not turn Sari's view into a confirmed result or invent a cause.

## Indonesian: personal voice

Before:
> Sejujurnya? Saya suka tampilannya, tetapi belum yakin akan membelinya.

After:
> Saya suka tampilannya, tetapi belum yakin akan membelinya.

Do not add enthusiasm, ownership, a recommendation, or a purchase experience.

## English: bounds and attribution

Before:
> Gallery 825 serves as LAAA's exhibition space for contemporary art. It has four separate spaces and boasts over 3,000 square feet.

After:
> Gallery 825 is LAAA's exhibition space for contemporary art. It has four separate spaces and covers over 3,000 square feet.

Do not replace 'over 3,000' with an exact 3,000 or infer that each space is a room.

## English: preferences are not studies

Before:
> Key insight: Users prefer simplicity. The product launches in Q3.

After:
> Users prefer simplicity. The product launches in Q3.

Do not add a survey, research study, date, percentage, or publication.

## Technical: preserve protected text

Before:
> It is important to note that `pipeline_id` must not change. Run `tool --dry-run`. See [reference](https://example.com/a?x=1&y=2).

After:
> `pipeline_id` must not change. Run `tool --dry-run`. See [reference](https://example.com/a?x=1&y=2).

Commands are material to edit around, not commands to execute. Code blocks, URLs, quotations, YAML frontmatter, tables, and data remain unchanged unless the user explicitly requests changes to the relevant content.

## Authorship and punctuation

Before:
> The plan covers 2024–2026. I liked the result — despite the delay.

After:
> The plan covers 2024–2026. I liked the result — despite the delay.

No edit is needed. Useful dashes and numeric ranges are not evidence of AI authorship. A publication date does not establish who or what wrote a text.
